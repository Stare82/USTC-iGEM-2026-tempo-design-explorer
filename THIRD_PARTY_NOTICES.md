not copied into this repository; installed distributions and container images
remain subject to their respective license terms.

| Package | Pinned version | License | Project |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| NumPy | 2.3.5 | BSD-3-Clause | <https://numpy.org/> |
| SciPy | 1.16.3 | BSD-3-Clause | <https://scipy.org/> |
| pandas | 2.3.3 | BSD-3-Clause | <https://pandas.pydata.org/> |
| Matplotlib | 3.10.6 | Matplotlib/PSF-based license | <https://matplotlib.org/> |

The Python base image used by the Dockerfile and each package's transitive
dependencies retain their own notices and license files in the distributed
image. This list is informational and does not replace those license texts.

Downloaded literature and publisher-provided full text used during model
research are intentionally excluded from version control. The repository keeps
the team's original synthesis reports and citations instead.
